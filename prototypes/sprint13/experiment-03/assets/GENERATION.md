## b-gouache.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Original wide 16:9 painted game environment design, Ben Vac fictional river landing inspired by ancient Vietnam. High oblique camera about 40 degrees above the ground, a readable 2.5D adventure/RPG game view, orthographic-like controlled perspective. Frame one compact working neighborhood: modest timber stilt house on upper bank, sloping earthen path down to rectangular wooden landing, a small timber boat hauled for repair on clear trestles to one side, a second boat in adjacent river, stacks of planks, baskets and drying nets, three small people with readable simple silhouettes doing ordinary work. Each object has a deliberate clear footprint and plausible joined geometry, path remains unblocked. River occupies one side as a quiet simple plane. Medium: handmade opaque gouache on subtly fibrous paper, flat matte pigment with deliberate brush edges, simple block-in shapes combined with selective tactile detail and subtle uneven pigment. Color direction materially different from cinematic fantasy: chalky grey-white light, soft slate blue water, pale earth/clay bank, muted leaf green, restrained vermilion cloth accents and dark plum-brown outlines. Keep colors discrete and chosen rather than tinting every material with one filter. Midday diffuse light, one simple consistent shadow direction, no golden glow, no orange/teal complementary wash, no blooming highlights, no gradients of digital airbrush, no photoreal textures, no glossy mobilegame 3D aesthetic. Sophisticated art for an adult narrative game, not cute chibi, not minimal vector diagram. Show enough negative space to read interactions but no game icons/UI/grid/text/numbers. Simple thatched roofs, not pagoda roofs; no towers, imperial ornaments, fantasy armor or modern clothing. Fictional art direction, not an archaeological claim. No border or watermark.
